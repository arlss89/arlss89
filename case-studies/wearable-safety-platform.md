# Wearable Safety Platform

## Overview

A cross-platform safety product concept that combines mobile navigation, wearable devices and location-based alerts.

## Engineering focus

- iOS application foundations
- Wearable data-field integration
- Kotlin/Spring Boot backend services
- Route and proximity processing
- Event-driven alert delivery
- Product development driven by a hardware proof of concept

## Architecture

The platform is divided into:

- Mobile client
- Wearable integration
- Backend API
- Incident and route domain
- Documentation and validation tooling

The system is designed to support incremental validation, starting with a focused wearable prototype before expanding the product surface.

## Engineering considerations

- Battery and connectivity constraints
- Limited wearable display and interaction capabilities
- Location accuracy and proximity thresholds
- Offline and delayed-event behavior
- Privacy-conscious retention of location-related data
- Clear separation between product assumptions and validated behavior

## Scope

This case study is intentionally generalized and contains no private source code, provider credentials, real locations or production integrations.
